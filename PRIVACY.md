# Chicago Management Bot — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 3 October 2026_

This policy covers the Chicago Management Discord bot ("the bot") only, run by the Chicago Roleplay staff team ("we"). It does not cover the Chicago Roleplay website, Discord, Roblox or ER:LC. See also the [Terms of Service](TERMS.md).

The bot stores only what it needs to run the Chicago Roleplay servers. We never sell your data.

## 1. What the bot collects

| Data | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Discord user ID, username, roles, join and leave events | You are in a server the bot runs in | Permissions, role sync, member counts, staff leave alerts |
| Infractions, bans and global bans (reason, moderator, time) | Staff take action against you | Moderation records |
| Staff application and ban appeal answers | You submit one | So staff can review it |
| Tickets (owner, claiming staff, time) and ticket transcripts | You open a ticket | Support and record keeping |
| Suggestions, suggestion votes, session votes, giveaway entries | You use those features | Counting votes and picking winners |
| AFK status, reason and old nickname; links to messages that mentioned you while AFK | You use `-afk` | Showing your AFK status and what you missed |
| Staff reviews (rating, reason) and donation totals | Someone submits a review, or a donation is recorded | Staff feedback and donor perks |
| Roblox username and ID, linked through Bloxlink | You verify or join the ER:LC server | Linking your Discord and Roblox accounts, group ranks |
| In-game ER:LC logs: joins, leaves, kills, commands | You play on the ER:LC server | In-game moderation |

The bot reads messages only to spot its own prefix commands (for example `-afk`) and AFK activity. It does not store message content.

## 2. Who it is shared with

- **Discord:** everything the bot does runs through Discord.
- **Roblox:** group rank changes and profile lookups.
- **Police Roleplay Community (ER:LC API):** reading in-game players and logs, sending in-game messages.
- **Bloxlink:** matching Discord accounts to Roblox accounts.
- **Cookie API:** creating ticket transcripts when a ticket is closed.
- **Staff:** Chicago Roleplay staff with the right role can see the records above.

We do not share data with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

## 3. Where it is stored and for how long

- Data is stored in a database on a private server hosted by Amazon Web Services in Stockholm, Sweden (eu-north-1). Only the bot owner can access it.
- Records are kept while the Chicago Roleplay servers run, or until you ask for them to be deleted. Moderation records may be kept after you leave so bans and infractions stay in force.

## 4. Your choices

- You can ask for a copy of your data, or for it to be corrected or deleted, by opening a ticket in the Chicago Roleplay Discord server.
- We may keep moderation records (bans, infractions) when deleting them would let rule breakers avoid action.
- You can stop new data being collected at any time by leaving the server.

## 5. Children

The bot is meant for users who meet Discord's minimum age. If you think a child below that age has given us data, open a ticket and we will delete it.

## 6. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

## 7. Contact

Questions about privacy: open a ticket in the Chicago Roleplay Discord server.
